～Welcome to My Website～

Hello! My name is Olivia, and I come from Chiayi, a city known for its pleasant weather and marvelous delicacies, such as tofu pudding and turkey rice. I am currently studying in the Department of Foreign Languages and Literature at National Tsing Hua University. I am an avid dog lover, and my favorite food is jelly and pudding. As for my personality traits, I consider myself friendly, patient, and meticulous. My friends often see me as a reliable and responsible teammate. They know they can always count on me when we work on group projects or plan trips together.

As a literature lover, I enjoy reading contemporary poems by Taiwanese writers, such as those by CHEN FAN-CHI and JEN, MING-HSIN, in my free time. I’m fascinated by the way their words strike a chord with me. Whenever I feel gloomy and down, these lines also provide comfort and remind me that I’m not alone. Additionally, I enjoy playing the ukulele to Western pop music, especially songs by Taylor Swift and Ed Sheeran. Strumming the strings and singing along to my favorite songs is how I let all my stress melt away. To me, music is an indispensable part of life.
